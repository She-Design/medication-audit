## 4. Cyprus context

*The local facts the product depends on.*

- **Duty pharmacy rota** — how off-hours coverage works, whether there is public
  data, and whether the app should surface it at all.
- **Over the counter** — what a household can buy without a prescription, since
  that determines what can realistically be in the kit checklist.
- **Naming** — the brand names households actually use, and the active
  ingredients behind them.
- **Heat** — indoor summer temperatures versus labelled storage limits, and what
  official guidance says about it.
- **Language** — the mix of Greek, English, Russian and Ukrainian in target
  households, and what that means for launch language.
